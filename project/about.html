About This Project

I've alwasys had a fascination with psychopomps and wanted to compile a little collection of them to compare and contrast.

Purpose

While this website was mostly made as an educational exercise in web development, hopefully anyone who visits will learn something new!

Scope & Limitations

  • Mythological and cultural interpretations vary widely
  • Entries are summaries, not exhaustive scholarship
  • Focused on a curated starting set of figures

References

The Bulk of information, and images comes from the Wikimedia Commons and from Wikipedia. Please consider supporting their efforts to make a free and accessible encyclopedia for everyone.

Built With

  • CSS Grid & Flexbox
